Bjorn International Brokerage is a global yacht brokerage and management house specialising in off-market and distressed sales, new construction and the resale of new-build positions for motor yachts of 50 to 120 metres.

Headquartered in Dubai, we advise owners, buyers and their family offices across North America, Asia, Europe and the Middle East, on transactions that are almost always conducted in private.
