SpecialismWhere timing and certainty matter most

Distressed and time-sensitive sales

We specialise in sales where timing and certainty matter most.

  • Owners needing liquidity
  • Lender- or court-driven sales
  • Estates
  • Corporate restructurings
  • Projects that have stalled

These transactions bring complexity: outstanding liens and mortgages, crew and supplier claims, flag and registration issues, and yachts laid up away from home port.

We work alongside specialist maritime lawyers, surveyors and financiers to resolve them and deliver a clean closing. For sellers, that means a fast, private exit. For buyers, it means access to significant value that the open market rarely offers.
