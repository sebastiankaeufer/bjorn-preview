A private brokerage,by design.

Bjorn International Brokerage is a global yacht brokerage and management house specialising in the sale, purchase, construction and management of motor yachts between 50 and 120 metres.

HeadquartersDubai, United Arab Emirates

Headquartered in Dubai, we work principally with private owners and family offices from North America and Asia, and the great majority of our work is never publicly listed.

Built on operating experience

Our roots are in owning and operating yachts, not only selling them.

The team’s experience goes back to Seven Yachts, which grew into the UAE’s largest day charter operation of company-owned vessels, based at the Bulgari Yacht Club on Jumeirah Bay Island. Building and running that fleet means we understand the full cost and reality of ownership, and advise accordingly.
