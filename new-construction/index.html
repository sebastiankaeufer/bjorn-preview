ProcessShipyard selection to delivery

Buyer representation

Building a yacht is a multi-year commitment involving hundreds of decisions and significant capital. We represent the buyer throughout, so that the yacht delivered is the yacht you set out to build.

  1. Brief & shipyard selection

    Defining how you will use the yacht, then identifying the yards, designers and platforms best suited to it, whether a proven series or full custom.

  2. Contract & specification

    Negotiating price, payment schedule, specification, warranty and delivery terms on your behalf.

  3. Design & build

    Coordinating with the shipyard, designers and your owner’s representative, with site visits and regular reporting through construction.

  4. Delivery & first season

    Sea trials, handover, crew and a smooth transition into ownership and, if you choose, into our management.

SpecialismBuying and selling before completion

Build positions

A specialism of ours is the private sale of positions in yachts still under construction.

Build positions

For owners

For owners whose plans have changed, we find a buyer for the contract before delivery, discreetly and without the yacht ever being publicly marketed.

Build positions

For buyers

For buyers, acquiring an existing build position can save years of waiting for a slot at a leading yard, often with the opportunity to influence the remaining specification and interior.

We manage the contract novation with the shipyard, the payment schedule already made, and the legal and technical due diligence on both sides.
