What we manage

Crew

Recruitment, contracts, payroll and rotation, with a captain and crew who understand how you use the yacht.

Technical & maintenance

Planned maintenance, surveys, class and flag requirements, and the management of refits and yard periods.

Financial

Annual budgets, cost control and clear, regular reporting.

Operations

Itinerary planning, provisioning, berthing and logistics across the Mediterranean, the Gulf and beyond.

Charter management

Where owners wish to offset running costs, we place the yacht with the right charter partners while protecting its condition and privacy.
